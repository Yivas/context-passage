# Code of Conduct

Context Passage welcomes respectful technical discussion and collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Stay on topic and provide enough detail for others to understand your point.
- Treat people with respect when they disagree or make a mistake.
- Give constructive feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Protect private information and use the security channel when disclosure could cause harm.
- Accept moderation decisions and stop an interaction when asked.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, trolling, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized content or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption, spam, or attempts to bypass moderation.
- Conduct that a reasonable person would consider unsafe in a professional project space.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. It also applies
when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this policy. Depending on the
severity and history of the behavior, the maintainer may issue a warning or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation.

Use GitHub's **Report content** action to report abuse, harassment, or private information. Do not
post private evidence in an issue. GitHub documents its confidential process in
[Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
